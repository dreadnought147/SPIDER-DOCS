# Questions for the Product Owner

Bring these to PO sessions. Record answers directly under each question.

---

## Payment

**Q1.** Which payment gateway has the business already evaluated or has a preference for?

**Q2.** What payment methods are required for Phase 1? (Card, EFT, SnapScan, etc.)

**Q3.** How do we handle delayed payment confirmation (e.g., EFT)? Do we hold stock? For how long?

## Vouchers

**Q4.** Fixed denominations or custom amounts for gift vouchers?

**Q5.** Can a voucher partially pay for an order, or must it cover the full amount?

**Q6.** One voucher per order, or can customers stack multiple codes?

**Q7.** Do vouchers expire? If yes, after how long?

## Delivery

**Q8.** Flat-rate delivery or regional fees?

**Q9.** Which logistics partner(s)? (Courier Guy, Aramex, Pargo, etc.)

**Q10.** Pickup points / locker collection — supported in Phase 1?

**Q11.** Delivery to all 9 provinces, or limited regions?

## Stock

**Q12.** Where does stock data live? Are we building inventory or integrating with an existing system?

**Q13.** What happens if a cart item goes out of stock during a session?

## Accounts

**Q14.** Is guest checkout ever planned, or is account-based a hard requirement?

**Q15.** Social login (Google/Facebook), or email/password only?

## Orders

**Q16.** What order statuses does the business need? (Pending, Paid, Shipped, etc.)

**Q17.** Can a customer cancel after payment? What's the refund flow?

**Q18.** Is an admin dashboard needed in Phase 1?

## Tech

**Q19.** Any preferences on tech stack?

**Q20.** Hosting budget or platform preference?

---

After each session: record the answer, update requirements, raise any new questions.
