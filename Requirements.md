# Comic Stock :  Phase 1 Requirements


## Business Requirements

- **BR-01** — The business must be able to do stock-based sales to ensure that customers can only purchase comic books that are currently in stock in the warehouse.
- **BR-02** — The business must be able to process customer payments through a secure third-party payment gateway to ensure that revenue can be generated from comic book sales.
- **BR-03** — The business must be able to operate through an account-based system to build a remarketing database for long-term customer engagement.
- **BR-04** — The business must be able to sell and redeem gift vouchers to capture the gift market and expand customer acquisition channels.
- **BR-05** — The business must be able to capture and validate delivery addresses and customer preferences to ensure successful order fulfillment.


---

## Functional Requirements

- **FR-01** — Customer acquisition centers on an account-based system.
- **FR-02** — Comic Stock will operate using a stock-based inventory model rather than drop-shipping.
- **FR-03** — The system must personalize recommendations.
- **FR-04** — The system enables customers to view, modify, and review items in their cart.
- **FR-05** — The system must process payments:
  - View, modify, and confirm cart items
  - Pay for cart items
  - Receive order confirmation
- **FR-06** — The system will support multiple payment methods.
- **FR-07** — The system must permit the use of voucher codes, both generation and redemption.
- **FR-08** — The system must capture and validate delivery addresses and customer preferences.
- **FR-09** — Incorporate social features that connect customers.

---

## Non-Functional Requirements

- **NFR-01** — Seamless user experience / purchasing items should be simple, easy and frictionless.
- **NFR-02** — Frictionless purchase journey.
- **NFR-03** — Greater control over quality and fulfillment speed — purchases and returns should be delivered quickly and reliably.
- **NFR-04** — Having full control over the order.
- **NFR-05** — Provision of a secure integration with a payment gateway — the system must be trustworthy and secure.
- **NFR-06** — The system must be legal.
